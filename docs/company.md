## About (from company LinkedIn, rewritten)

IoT-AI.Tech applies a systematic technology-application selection approach to turn a sovereign, local-first AI-IoT factory architecture into validated industrial, regulated and commercially scalable use cases for the German and European market.

Priority areas named on LinkedIn:

1. **AI agent control and evidence plane** — governance and execution for agentic AI: context graphs, policy control, human approval, audit trails and operational evidence.
2. **Sovereign local AI compute and learning control plane** — local-first data validation, model evaluation, adapter training and controlled capability release without unnecessary data egress.
3. **Industrial edge and operations intelligence** — modular dashboards and command surfaces for industrial operations, fleet and asset visibility, predictive maintenance and project intelligence.

The company looks for pilot partners, system integrators, research collaborators, public-funding partners and investors who want trustworthy, sovereign and auditable AI systems.
