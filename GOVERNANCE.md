# Governance

IoT-AI.Tech is the project steward and final release authority.

- Community source is developed in public-facing, privacy-safe boundaries.
- Enterprise components, customer policies, entitlement issuer tools and private evidence are maintained separately.
- Technical completion is distinct from founder/release acceptance.
- Security, public-boundary and deterministic test gates can block a release.
- Release history is append-only; superseded artifacts are not silently replaced.
