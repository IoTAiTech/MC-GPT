# NIS2 customer alignment

## Claim boundary

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 applies according to entity type, size, sector and national transposition. IOT-AI provides supporting controls and evidence; it does not determine that every customer is in scope or legally compliant.

Official sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj/eng
- https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/nis2-technical-implementation-guidance

## Supporting controls

- risk and supplier inventory through Enterprise ProductX/AIMDB integration;
- least privilege, explicit tool authority and tenant boundaries;
- package/software supply-chain manifests and SBOM;
- vulnerability and incident evidence;
- backup, restore and normal rollback drills;
- business-continuity-friendly side-by-side updates;
- log, correlation and diagnostic exports;
- cryptographic integrity and replay-resistant approvals;
- public/private/customer data separation;
- provider/model route health and failure classification.

Customers remain responsible for entity classification, management accountability, national reporting routes and applicable risk-management measures.
