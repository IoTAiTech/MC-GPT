# Article 50 direct-interaction transparency

Every human-facing CLI, Meeting, Mesh, Multi-Coder or agentic run records a first-interaction notice in English, German or Persian. The notice identifies IoT-AI.Tech as operator, warns that output may be inaccurate/incomplete, and states that material actions require human approval.

The receipt stores no raw personal identity. It binds the surface, language, Suite version, disclosure version, timestamp and disclosure-text hash.

Live customer interfaces must independently prove visible, distinguishable and accessible first-interaction display. A source-code implementation alone is not a live-fleet conformity claim.
