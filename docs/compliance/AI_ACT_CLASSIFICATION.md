# AI Act classification policy

The public Developer Preview is assessed as an interactive/generative AI system, not high-risk for its declared software-development purpose. This is a technical triage, not a binding legal opinion.

## Reassessment triggers

- intended-purpose or customer-sector change;
- safety, critical-infrastructure, medical, employment, education, essential-service, law-enforcement, migration, justice or biometric use;
- model, prompt/policy, RAG source, tool authority or autonomy change;
- customer rebranding or substantial modification;
- material change in affected persons or deployment jurisdiction.

A high-risk candidate is not execution-eligible until an authorised legal-technical reviewer records the Article 6/Annex I/Annex III rationale and the applicable control pack.
