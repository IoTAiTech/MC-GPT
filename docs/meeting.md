# Meeting Engine

A meeting is an execution-graph template, not a free-form chat room. Required phases are knowledge coverage, independent role opinions, blind challenge, layered fan-in, frozen plan digest and exact-digest acceptance.

A successful meeting must include direct answer, 5W1H, architecture/plan, KPI/SLA, ten use cases, ten test cases, ten failure cases, risks, disagreements, missing evidence and a substantive acceptance matrix.
