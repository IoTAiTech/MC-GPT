# Public/private/customer boundary

Public, private Enterprise and customer knowledge/evidence use physically separate roots and repositories. Public export is allowlist-built; symlinks, private keys, secrets, private addresses, internal hostnames, personal paths, customer data, Enterprise source, runtime databases and private evidence are forbidden.

Raw prompts/outputs are not stored by default. Diagnostics exports are redacted and hash-verified. Supplier credentials remain in provider-native sessions or secret stores and never in settings, Git, task titles, meeting topics, evidence metadata or provenance sidecars.
